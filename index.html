Our story

A neighbourhood salon that grew up with its guests

Bloom & Blush started in 2011 as a two-chair studio above a bakery, with one rule that hasn't changed: nobody gets rushed.

Today our team of eleven works out of a light-filled space on Blossom Street, but the pace is still deliberately unhurried. We book longer appointments than most salons because a good consultation saves a bad colour correction — and because the chair should feel like the calmest half of your week.

Every stylist trains four days a year with visiting educators, we're a fully cruelty-free product house, and we refill and recycle everything the city will take.
